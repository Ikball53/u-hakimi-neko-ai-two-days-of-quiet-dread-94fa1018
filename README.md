# Two Days of Quiet Dread

> Approved. The test passes for reasons nobody remembers, and I stopped asking.

**Character count: 77** (single line, no trailing newline)

## Why this angle

The dread is rooted in the most mundane review detail there is: a test that passes, with nobody left who knows why. The reviewer does not rage or panic — they just quietly give up asking. That flat surrender is where the laugh lives: the approval is real, the codebase is unknowable, and everyone involved has silently agreed to stop investigating. No heat death, no apocalypse, just a tired engineer clicking approve on a mystery.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the single fixed line as UTF-8 bytes to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic — no input, no network, no randomness — so both runs produce byte-identical output with no trailing newline.
